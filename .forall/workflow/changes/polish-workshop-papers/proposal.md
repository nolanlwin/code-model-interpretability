# Proposal: polish-workshop-papers

## Why

The LP4FM and Interp as a Science workshop manuscripts need publication-quality academic prose, accurate venue positioning, verified citations and claims, and strict compliance with their four-page and five-page main-text limits.

## What Changes

- Revise both manuscripts for precise, professional AI safety and mechanistic interpretability writing.
- Preserve the authors' actual evidence, results, anonymity, and institutional provenance without inventing credentials or affiliations.
- Fact-check venue requirements, citations, model descriptions, numerical claims, and contribution framing against primary or authoritative sources.
- Rebuild both PDFs and ensure references begin after the applicable main-text page limit.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- polished-workshop-manuscripts
- verified-academic-claims
- workshop-page-limit-compliance

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Verification scope

The prose and document layout are spec-tracked rather than formally proved. Citation and claim checks use source-backed review, while page limits and LaTeX compilation use executable checks.

## Impact

The change affects `lp4fm_short/main.tex`, `interp4d_short/main.tex`, and generated PDFs. Bibliography files may be corrected only when fact-checking identifies a concrete citation defect. No application APIs or runtime dependencies change.
