\paragraph{Measurement protocol (boolean role).}
Programs come from XLCoST \citep{zhu2022xlcost} in \emph{detokenized}
form: the official release is TransCoder-tokenized (whitespace-mangled),
so we reconstruct natural source and validate that each program parses
before use. Every variable occurrence carries a stable identity
$\langle\textit{problem},\textit{language},\textit{function},
\textit{binding},\textit{occurrence}\rangle$ assigned at extraction; all
paired comparisons join on these identities, never on character offsets.
Activations are mean-pooled residual streams at the occurrence's own
tokens, extracted with one forward pass per program; token--character
alignment is verified per model by an offset gate before any extraction
(a roundtrip check alone is insufficient: one candidate tokenizer
round-tripped correctly while corrupting half its offsets).

Splits are grouped at the \emph{problem} level and frozen by hash: each
problem is assigned to train/validation/test ($70/10/20$) by a
deterministic digest of its identifier and the seed, so every
counterfactual condition tests on the same problems regardless of which
programs survive rewriting. Probes are $\ell_2$-regularized logistic
regressions on standardized features, fit per layer; the reported layer is
selected on the \emph{validation} fold and evaluated once on test, for
each of five seeds. Classes falling below a support threshold are dropped
explicitly and reported, never silently. Occurrence counts are capped at
$2{,}000$ per (role, language) by whole-problem sampling with a fixed
seed; the sampled identity list is frozen and reused by every baseline
and condition. Baselines share this population exactly; counterfactual
conditions share it less whatever their rewrite legitimately cannot
produce (Section~\ref{sec:boolean} reports the retained share per
condition), and paired comparisons are refused outright if fewer than
$90\%$ of the smaller fold's occurrences match.

Each probe is reported against model-free baselines computed on the
identical sample and fold: majority class, a classifier over the variable
name alone, length covariates, a masked context window, and a masked
enclosing source line. Probe capacity is checked with a control task in
the sense of \citet{hewitt2019control} (a random label per variable name,
drawn from the empirical marginal), reported as selectivity. Uncertainty
is quantified by BCa bootstrap over \emph{problems} (occurrences within a
program are dependent), and renaming effects are paired deltas on shared
test occurrences pooled across seeds, with the comparison refused
outright if fewer than $90\%$ of the smaller fold's occurrences match.

Renaming counterfactuals are produced by scope-correct span edits on the
original source---never re-pretty-printing---so formatting, comments, and
strings are untouched; renames respect binding scope, avoid collisions,
and are seeded deterministically per program and condition. Rewritten
programs must re-parse, and extraction on the rewritten program must
recover an occurrence at every mapped span with an unchanged label;
programs failing any gate are dropped and counted. Functions whose scopes
the rewriter cannot handle exactly (nested scopes, \texttt{global}
declarations, \texttt{except}-as binders) are left unrenamed and counted
rather than approximated.
