\label{sec:boolean}
For boolean-control variables we probe the syntactic \emph{occurrence type}
of each flag site---assignment, conditional use, loop use, or return
use---from mean-pooled residual activations at the flag's own tokens, on
XLCoST Python under the protocol of Section~3: problem-grouped frozen
splits, validation-selected layers, five seeds, and every probe reported
against model-free baselines on the identical occurrence sample.

\paragraph{Occurrence type is decodable at ceiling---and so is the source
line.} Across three models spanning two families and a $4.5\times$ scale
range, probes reach macro-F1 $0.977$--$0.989$ against a majority baseline
of $0.174$ (Table~\ref{tab:boolean-baselines}). Selectivity over a
Hewitt-style control task \citep{hewitt2019control} is positive and stable
($+0.189$ to $+0.203$), so the probe is reading representation rather than
memorising at this sample size. However, a character $n$-gram classifier
over the \emph{enclosing source line} with the variable masked---no model
involved---reaches macro-F1 $0.988$ on the same occurrences and fold. Two
of the three probes fall below that line ($-0.011$ and $-0.003$) and the
third exceeds it by $+0.0005$, a margin far inside seed variation. Nor
does the probe improve with code pretraining ($0.977 \to 0.985$ at fixed
scale) or with $4.5\times$ more parameters ($0.989$). Occurrence type is
carried by local syntax that is fully present in the surface form; linear
decodability here is not evidence of computation beyond it. A static
analyser decides this label exactly, so the question of interest is not
whether the information is present but \emph{what carries it}---which the
renaming counterfactuals isolate.

\paragraph{Renaming does not move the probe.} We re-extract activations
for five naming counterfactuals applied by scope-correct span edits with
occurrence identities preserved (Section~3). Each condition is drawn from
the same frozen occurrence sample as the baseline, less the occurrences
its rewrite legitimately cannot produce: the rewriter refuses functions
whose scopes it cannot handle exactly and drops programs failing the
re-parse or identity gates, retaining $98.5\%$ of the sample for C1/C2/C4
and $97.7\%$ for C3/C5. Attrition is structural---driven by program scope
constructs, not by the label or the probe's behaviour---and paired deltas
are computed on the resulting shared intersection, with the comparison
refused outright below $90\%$ overlap.

Table~\ref{tab:boolean-renaming} reports those deltas. Every one is within
$\pm 0.016$ macro-F1, across all five conditions and all three models---
including C5, which renames each flag to an index-style identifier
(\texttt{i}, \texttt{idx}, \texttt{pos}), the same manipulation that costs
the index role $\Delta\mathrm{F1} = -0.272$ (Section~\ref{sec:index}). The
deltas do not share a sign: they scatter around zero (median $-0.003$),
which is the signature of seed noise rather than a suppressed effect.
Boolean occurrence type is therefore \textbf{name-invariant}: the signal
survives complete removal, collapse, or adversarial inversion of lexical
information, because it never depended on it.

\paragraph{Placement on the dissociation axis.} The three roles now occupy
distinct, replicated positions under one corpus and one manipulation:
index collapses under misleading names ($\Delta\mathrm{F1}=-0.272$),
accumulator improves ($+0.079$), and boolean is invariant
($|\Delta\mathrm{F1}| \le 0.016$ across three models). Combined with the
baseline analysis above, the boolean case also sharpens the interpretation
of the axis itself: name-invariance alone does not imply an abstract role
representation---here the invariant signal is local syntax, available in
the enclosing line and decodable from the earliest layers. Cross-role
comparisons of probe F1 should therefore be read jointly with surface
baselines, not against majority chance alone.

A Java replication (XLCoST, same protocol) shows the same profile---probes
at $0.981$--$0.990$ with information complete by layer~5, matched by a
masked \emph{statement} baseline at $0.990$ (Java's detokenized programs
are single-line, so the enclosing statement rather than the line is the
local-surface unit). Its heavily skewed class distribution (majority
$0.859$; \texttt{loop\_use} absent from the sample) leaves less headroom,
and we report it as secondary. Renaming counterfactuals are Python-only
(Section~3).
